HOW TRACERRR WORKS

Your audience, turned into a newsletter business.

  1. 01

    Reach.

    We turn your followers into subscribers: a newsletter in your voice, with sign-ups built into your videos, socials and site.

    Creator audiences that chose to subscribe, in golf, cricket and more, reached in the inbox rather than the feed.

  2. 02

    Relationship.

    We write, design and send every edition, and grow the list. You record a voice note and approve. That's it.

    Readers who open every week, because it comes from someone they already trust. Your brand sits inside that habit.

  3. 03

    Revenue.

    We sell and run the sponsorships. You approve every partner, and you earn when it works.

    Labelled, creator-approved placements across one title or the whole network, with reporting after every send.
