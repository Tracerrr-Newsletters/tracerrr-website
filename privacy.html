Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This policy explains what information Tracerrr collects when you use tracerrr.com, why we collect it, and the choices you have. We keep it short and plain.

Who we are

Tracerrr ("we", "us") operates a network of creator-led newsletters and connects brands with those audiences. For any privacy question, contact us at jake@tracerrr.com. For the purposes of UK data protection law, Tracerrr is the data controller for information collected through this website.

What we collect

  • Information you give us. When you submit our enquiry form or email us, we collect your name, email address, whether you're a brand or a creator, and any message you send.
  • Usage information. Like most websites, we use analytics to understand how visitors find and use the site — for example pages viewed, approximate location (country/region), device and browser type, and how you arrived (including from advertising). This is collected via cookies and similar technologies.

How we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry and discuss working together.
  • To measure and improve the performance of the website and our advertising.
  • To keep records of our business communications.

Our lawful bases are your consent (for non-essential analytics/advertising cookies), our legitimate interests in running and improving our business, and taking steps to enter into a contract at your request when you make an enquiry.

Cookies and analytics

We use Google Analytics to produce aggregate statistics about site usage. Analytics cookies are only set where permitted. You can control or block cookies through your browser settings; doing so won't stop you using the site.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run the site and communicate with you — such as our analytics provider (Google), our email and scheduling tools, and our website host — and only as needed to provide those services. Some of these providers may process data outside the UK/EEA under appropriate safeguards.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for as long as needed to deal with your request and for our legitimate business records, and then delete or anonymise them. Analytics data is retained according to our provider's standard retention periods.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict our use of your personal data, to object to processing, and to data portability. To exercise any of these, email jake@tracerrr.com. You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk) if you're unhappy with how we've handled your data.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date above shows when it last changed.

Questions about this policy? Email jake@tracerrr.com.
